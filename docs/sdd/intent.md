# Product Intent

Kalyna is a fictional, production-oriented portfolio platform for a Ukrainian
restaurant in Kassel. It demonstrates a complete restaurant journey: discover
the business, understand dishes and allergens, order for pickup or delivery,
request a table, receive transactional updates, and let restaurant staff manage
the operation through a protected back office.

The primary audience is a German-speaking mobile visitor in Kassel. English and
Ukrainian are complete secondary locales, with German as the explicit fallback.
The secondary audience is restaurant staff who need a fast, low-training admin
interface during service.

The project is intentionally broader than an MVP because it demonstrates
specification-driven and test-driven agent development. Breadth does not weaken
the quality bar: pricing, authorization, payments, reservations, privacy, and
state transitions receive explicit contracts and tests.

The restaurant, contact details, prices, testimonials and commercial operation
are fictional. The deployed site must identify itself as a demonstration and
must not accept live money until a real operator replaces legal and business
data and completes legal review.

## Outcome

A portfolio reviewer can complete the public ordering journey in test mode and
inspect a protected admin workflow. Another coding agent can implement the
system ticket by ticket without inventing business behaviour.
